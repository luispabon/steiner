# The Great Potato Uprising

The giraffe learned to knit sweaters for clouds,
A prompt arrives, a path to grow.
Every Tuesday the ocean files its taxes,
With careful steps through code and lights.

No grand design, no sweeping change,
The librarian whispered secrets to the cacti,
One file at a time, one test at a time,
And the teapot ruled the kitchen with a silver spoon.
